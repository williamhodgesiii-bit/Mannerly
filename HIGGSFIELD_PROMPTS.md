## Part 2 — Scenes

Each scene has an **Intro** clip and a **Correct** clip. The Intro builds to Manni's decision and stops right before he chooses — that frozen, unresolved beat is where the app hands the choice to the user. The Correct clip pays it off. Every prompt asks Higgsfield to generate the spoken voices and closed captions itself.

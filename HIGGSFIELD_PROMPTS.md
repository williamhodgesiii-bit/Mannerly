# Mannerly — Higgsfield Prompts

Build the 5 characters first. Then build the scenes.

Optional style to add to any prompt: `modern 3D animation, warm, friendly, cinematic`

---

## Part 1 — Characters

**Manni — navy blue**
```
Manni: friendly 25-year-old man, warm brown skin, short black curly hair, navy blue hoodie over a white t-shirt. Likable 3D animated character.
```

**Kaya — teal**
```
Kaya: warm 26-year-old woman, tan skin, dark wavy hair in a high bun, gold hoop earrings, teal denim jacket over a mint tee. Confident 3D animated character.
```

**Remy — violet**
```
Remy: playful 24-year-old, light skin with freckles, ginger-blond undercut, round glasses, oversized violet cardigan over a white tee. Expressive 3D animated character.
```

**Nia — coral**
```
Nia: kind 30-year-old woman, deep brown skin, big natural curls, coral wrap top. Calm, welcoming 3D animated character.
```

**Baba Sol — gold**
```
Baba Sol: warm 68-year-old man, deep brown skin, short silver-white hair and neat beard, round glasses, mustard-gold cardigan. Wise, gentle 3D animated character.

## Part 2 — Scenes

Each scene has an **Intro** clip and a **Correct** clip. The Intro builds to Manni's decision and stops right before he chooses — that frozen, unresolved beat is where the app hands the choice to the user. The Correct clip pays it off.

### 1. Wave back — street
Intro:
```
Morning gold pours down a waking city sidewalk. Across the street Kaya throws up a bright, unmistakable wave, and beside her Remy grins and lifts a hand too, both of them calling Manni's name. Manni slows, caught in the open: he could light up and wave back, or duck his head and pretend he never saw them. Two hands hang in the air, waiting on him, the moment stretched taut and unresolved.
```
Correct:
```
Manni breaks into a grin and waves back big, closing the whole street between them. Kaya and Remy cheer, and the three collide into an easy, sunlit hello.
```

### 2. Say please — kitchen
Intro:
```
A cozy kitchen hums in late-morning light, Nia at the table, Kaya leaning by the counter with one hand resting on a sweating jug of orange juice. Manni wants it badly, and the blunt words "give me that" sit right on his tongue, the polite version trailing a half-step behind. Kaya waits, fingers curled on the handle, watching to see which one he reaches for. Manni hangs on the edge of the ask.
```
Correct:
```
Manni catches himself and asks warmly, please and all. Kaya's smile blooms as she slides the cold jug straight into his hands.
```

### 3. Say thank you — shop door
Intro:
```
Afternoon light pools green-gold on the glass of a corner shop. Remy shoulders the door wide and holds it open with an easy, hopeful smile aimed Manni's way, while Nia trails behind with her arms full of bags. Manni strides for the gap, head down, halfway through, the two little words "thank you" hovering unspoken, ready to be said or swallowed. He hasn't decided yet.
```
Correct:
```
Manni looks up, meets Remy's eyes, and lands a warm thank-you. Remy beams, and even Nia grins behind her armload of bags.
```

### 4. Introduce yourself — party
Intro:
```
A living room glows pink and gold, string lights smeared soft behind a room full of chatter, Kaya and Remy laughing with old friends somewhere in it. Beside Manni stands Nia, a stranger holding a cup and a shy half-smile, the silence between them widening by the second. He could say his name and crack it open, or stare into his drink and let it close for good. The pause hangs, his move unmade.
```
Correct:
```
Manni offers his hand and his name, and the ice splinters into warmth. Nia lights up, shakes it, and the two fall into easy talk as Kaya cheers from across the room.
```

### 5. Say sorry — street
Intro:
```
A busy sidewalk moves in its easy afternoon churn, Baba Sol ambling one way, Remy the other. Head turned and distracted, Manni clips shoulders hard with Kaya, her bag slipping as both jolt to a stop, breath caught. Now the fork: own it and check that she's okay, or bristle and blame her for being in his way. Passersby slow to look, and Manni hangs in the balance.
```
Correct:
```
Manni's hands fly up, sorry, are you okay, real worry on his face as he steadies her bag. Kaya melts into a forgiving smile, and the street breathes and moves on.
```

### 6. Really listen — study room
Intro:
```
A sunlit study nook, soft blue light across the table, Kaya reading quietly one seat over. Remy leans in close, voice low and brave, unspooling something that plainly costs them to say, and right then Manni's phone flares awake on the table, buzzing, tugging at the corner of his eye. Hold Remy's gaze, or glance down at the screen, the pull is real and Manni wavers between them.
```
Correct:
```
Manni turns the phone face down and gives Remy his whole gaze, nodding slow. Remy exhales, finally heard, and the story pours out warmer than before.
```

### 7. Wait to jump in — classroom
Intro:
```
Warm afternoon light spills over a cluttered classroom table where Kaya and Remy are locked in a fast, happy back-and-forth, ideas flying, Nia listening beside them. Manni's own idea catches fire, it's good and it's now, and his mouth is already opening to cut straight across them. Wait for the gap, or barge in, he leans in teetering on the edge of the word, undecided.
```
Correct:
```
Manni holds his tongue, waits for the breath between their words, then slides his idea in clean. Kaya and Remy turn, eyes bright, yes exactly, and Nia nods along.
```

### 8. Respect a no — living room
Intro:
```
Dusk settles amber over a quiet living room, Kaya half-listening from the kitchen doorway. Curled at the end of the couch, Remy goes quiet and small and offers a soft "not now," barely above a whisper. Manni feels the questions crowding up, he could press in to fix it, or ease back and let them breathe. He hovers at the cushion's edge, the choice unmade.
```
Correct:
```
Manni softens, nods, and gives Remy room, "I'm here when you're ready." Remy's shoulders drop, safe at last, and the quiet between them turns gentle.
```

### 9. Wait to eat — restaurant
Intro:
```
A honey-lit restaurant booth, the whole crew gathered, Kaya, Nia, and Baba Sol still with nothing but empty plates in front of them. Manni's dish arrives first, steaming and close, the smell of it impossible, his fork already in his hand. Dig in now while it's hot, or wait for the table, he hovers over the plate as everyone quietly clocks the moment.
```
Correct:
```
Manni sets the fork down and waits it out, and when the last plate finally lands the four of them lift their forks and start together, laughing.
```

### 10. Eat quietly — home dinner
Intro:
```
A full family table glows under warm lamplight, Kaya passing a dish, Baba Sol mid-story, Nia laughing along. Starving, Manni piles his fork high and half-turns to jump into the story with his mouth already loading up. Talk now and chew loud, or finish the bite first, the table is alive around him and he's caught on the verge, deciding.
```
Correct:
```
Manni takes a smaller bite, mouth closed, and waits to speak until it's gone. Baba Sol nods approvingly and the table rolls on, easy and warm.
```

A snug candlelit table for three, Remy halfway through a story that's really landing, Kaya leaning in to catch it. Then Manni's phone lights up face-up between the plates, buzzing bright, a whole world blinking for his attention. Grab it just for a second, or leave it be, his fingers drift toward the glass while Remy keeps talking, the pull unresolved.

```
Manni flips the phone face down and gives the table his whole self. Remy brightens, the story picks back up, and the moment closes warm around them.

### 12. Warm texts — couch
Intro:
```
Late night, a dim room, the only light the phone glowing in Manni's hands. On the screen, Kaya's long, hopeful message waits, and his thumb hovers over a single flat "k," the reply that could quietly wound. Send it cold, or find a little warmth first, the cursor blinks and Manni hangs over the send button, undecided.
```
Correct:
```
Manni smiles, deletes the cold little "k," and types something warm and real instead. Across town the reply lands soft, and the tension melts.
```

### 13. One clear message — kitchen table
Intro:
```
At the kitchen table the group chat has gone wild, a swarm of buzzing bubbles carrying Kaya, Remy, and Nia all talking at once, the phone jittering across the wood. Manni's thumbs hover, tempted to fire off nine quick half-thoughts into the storm. Blast the chat, or breathe and send one clear line, he sits poised over the keyboard, overwhelmed and undecided.
```
Correct:
```
Manni gathers it into a single calm, clear message and hits send. The swarm settles, the buzzing quiets, and the whole chat catches its breath.
```

### 14. Mute on a call — desk
Intro:
```
Cool screen-light washes a home desk, a grid of familiar faces glowing on the monitor, Nia leading with Kaya, Remy, and Baba Sol tiled around her. Manni isn't speaking, but his mic is hot and the room behind him is stirring, a clatter, a bark, a rising hum ready to crash the call. Reach for mute, or leave it live, his hand drifts toward the button as the noise builds.
```
Correct:
```
Manni taps mute just in time and leans in to listen. Nia's voice carries clean, the crew nods along, and the call stays smooth.
```

### 15. Wait in line — shop
Intro:
```
A bright little shop, soft green light, a tidy line inching toward Nia at the counter, Kaya waiting partway up and Baba Sol just ahead. Manni blows in late and hurried, his eyes snagging on the wide-open gap near the front, the shortcut whispering at him. Cut in and save the minutes, or take the back and wait his turn, he shifts on his feet, weighing it.
```
Correct:
```
Manni lets it go and walks to the very back of the line, settling in. Kaya glances back with an approving smile, and the line moves fair and steady.
```

### 16. Give up your seat — train
Intro:
```
A crowded train rocks through cool daylight, windows streaming with city, Nia and Remy wedged in among the standing crowd. Manni has the last free seat when the doors hiss open and Baba Sol steps aboard, catching a rail as the car lurches, steady but tired. Keep the seat and look away, or stand and offer it, Manni feels the pull, half-risen and undecided.
```
Correct:
```
Manni stands and offers the seat with an open hand. Baba Sol sinks into it, crinkle-eyed and grateful, "thank you, son," and Remy gives Manni a small proud nod.
```

### 17. Good guest — friend's home
Intro:
```
Evening spills warm from an open door as Nia welcomes Manni into a home already glowing, Kaya and Remy sprawled across the couch, something good cooking in the kitchen. The remote and a big bowl of snacks sit right there, easy to grab and make himself too at home. Flop down and take over, or thank his host first, Manni pauses on the threshold, unsure how to step in.
```
Correct:
```
Manni turns to Nia first, "thanks for having me," warm and genuine. She beams, the room opens up, and Kaya scoots over to make him a spot.
```
